* Feature: The app runs on Apple TV, and Remote Access lets you set it up from a phone or computer on the same network
* Update: UDP has a new protocol; on a server that has not updated to it yet, the app connects without UDP
* Improve: The app draws its screens itself rather than as a web page, and text is easier to read, with more contrast
* Feature: The report has a Share button on phones and tablets, a Copy button on a computer and a Download button in a browser on Remote Access, so it is easy to send to support
* Feature: An access key's file (.cdy) and its link (vh:...) open in VpnHood! CLIENT, which adds its server, chooses it and says so
* Update: Access keys start with vh: rather than vh://, so their links open on iPhone and Mac too; a key given out before still works everywhere, and as a link everywhere but on iPhone and Mac
* Update: On a plan with a speed limit, the first megabytes after a pause go at full speed, so pages load quickly, and only steady heavy use is held to the limit
* Feature: In Cloak Mode, the Protocols page has Block UDP in other apps, which stops all UDP except DNS; voice and video calls and games that need UDP stop working with it
* Update: The Protocols page tells two QUICs apart: the switch is now Block QUIC in other apps, and TCP, UDP and QUIC sit under a heading of their own, Tunnel protocol
* Fix: The home page shows CLOAK whenever Cloak Mode is on, also when the server or the Split Domain settings turn it on, and no longer shows it when the server keeps it off
* Fix: Split Local Network does what it says: on, local devices such as a printer or a TV are reached directly; off, their traffic stays in the VPN. It did the opposite while Allow Split Tunneling was off, or on a server that carries local addresses
* Fix: A VPN started without opening the app - always-on, the quick-settings tile, the phone's VPN settings, or the system restarting it - follows your latest settings, "Share anonymous usage data" among them, and the server and location you chose, never a server you removed
* Fix: "Check for update" always answers - you have the latest version, or it could not check - and shows the update's notice even after you chose Later
* Fix: The app's language can be set in your device's settings as well as in the app, and each shows the other's choice: the app's page in Settings has a Language row when the device has more than one language
* Fix: When the app was updated while connected, it now opens on Disconnected instead of saying "VPN service encountered an issue"
* Fix: Your settings and servers now go into your phone's backup and come back on a new phone, while your sign-in, the app's logs and its unpacked files stay on the phone they belong to
* Fix: A big upload over a slow connection no longer holds up the device's other traffic, such as browsing
* Fix: Online games that send large packets, such as Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 on Steam, work through the VPN; every packet over 1280 bytes was dropped. A VpnHoodServer of your own needs this release too
* Improve: When a server on a dynamic DNS name gets a new address while you are connected, the app finds the new address and connects again by itself, unless it is set to UDP
